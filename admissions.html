Admissions

Four steps from a question to a seat.

Enrolment is a conversation first. Tell us the programme or the role you are aiming at, and counselling covers the rest before anything is committed.

The enrolment process

  1. STEP 01

    Enquire

    Send the form below, or call. Name the programme or the role — either is enough.

  2. STEP 02

    Counselling

    We go through the programme scope, the training areas, the learning mode and the timetable.

  3. STEP 03

    Enrol

    Complete the institute's enrolment requirements and confirm your batch.

  4. STEP 04

    Train & certify

    Attend the training and complete the institute requirements for your certificate.
