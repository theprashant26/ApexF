Four steps from a question to a seat.

Enrolment is a conversation first. Tell us the programme or the role you are aiming at, and counselling covers the rest before anything is committed.

The enrolment process

  1. STEP 01

    Enquire

    Send the form below, or call. Name the programme or the role — either is enough.

  2. STEP 02

    Counselling

    We go through the programme scope, the training areas, the learning mode and the timetable.

  3. STEP 03

    Enrol

    Complete the institute's enrolment requirements and confirm your batch.

  4. STEP 04

    Train & certify

    Attend the training and complete the institute requirements for your certificate.

All Apex programmes are delivered online. There is no classroom attendance requirement, so you can study from anywhere. Assignments and assessments are completed remotely, and because there is no fixed intake you can enrol at any time and begin with the next available session.

On successfully completing the prescribed training programme and fulfilling the applicable academic requirements, eligible learners may be awarded a Course Completion Certificate by Apex Professional Academy, identifying the programme completed and its programme code. It is not a government certificate, a university degree, a statutory licence or an accredited qualification.

Career guidance, interview preparation and resume/CV assistance may form part of a programme's common features, subject to institute requirements. Completion of a training programme does not automatically guarantee employment, appointment, government recruitment, professional registration or a particular salary.
