1. About this website

This website is operated by Apex Professional Academy, which functions under Apex Institute of Multidisciplinary Professional Studies (short name: APA / AIMPS). The Academy is a professional skill development and career-oriented training institute. This site describes its training programmes, the areas each programme covers and the career-oriented roles each programme trains for.

3. Certificates and recognition

Apex Professional Academy clearly distinguishes its own training certificates from government, university, statutory-board or professional-licensing qualifications. Any claim of affiliation, approval, accreditation or recognition is published only when supported by the applicable official authorisation or agreement.

Where no such statement appears against a programme on this website, no such affiliation, approval, accreditation or recognition is being claimed. The Academy's registration status is {{PLACEHOLDER: legal / registration status}}.

4. Career-oriented roles

Every programme on this website lists the career-oriented roles it trains for. These are descriptions of the kind of work the training prepares a student for. They are not job offers, vacancies or placement commitments, and they carry no salary claim.

Completion of a training programme does not automatically guarantee employment, appointment, government recruitment, professional registration or a particular salary.

Career guidance, interview preparation and resume/CV assistance may form part of a programme's common features, subject to institute requirements.

    10. Data privacy and record retention

    Apex Professional Academy may collect and process information submitted by students and prospective students for legitimate institutional purposes, including:

      What we collect

      When you submit an enquiry or contact form we collect the details you enter: your name, phone number, email address, city, the programme you selected, your preferred learning mode and your message.

      What we do not do

      • We do not sell your details.
      • We do not share them with third parties except where required by law.
      • We do not use them for unrelated marketing without your consent.

      Your rights

      Cookies
