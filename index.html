How enrolment works

    Getting started

    How enrolment works

    Four steps, in order, from your first question to structured training.

    1. STEP 01

      Enquire

      Tell us the programme or the role you are interested in. A short form is enough to start.

    2. STEP 02

      Counselling

      We go through the programme scope, the learning mode and what the training actually covers.

    3. STEP 03

      Enrol

      Complete the institute's enrolment requirements and confirm your batch.

    4. STEP 04

      Train & certify

      Take part in the training and complete the institute requirements for your certificate.
